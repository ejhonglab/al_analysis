    Over time frame by frame, both RDMs at the same time

      How to read this

      • Y beyond E[Y | X] is z(Y) minus the least-squares fit of z(Y) on z(X), linear or quadratic, over the pairs both RDMs have. It is uncorrelated with z(X) by construction. X→Y change is z(Y) − z(X); it equals the residual minus (1 − slope)·z(X), so it carries a regression-to-the-mean term and correlates negatively with X.
      • Odor part: the least-squares additive fit ai + aj to the centred values (as in pair_movers.py); positive ai means odor i is more similar to all the others in RDM 2 than expected. Pair part: what remains. The two are orthogonal, so their variance shares add to 100%.
      • Time-binned RDMs are fly means per frame (0.25 s). A window averages the frames with a ≤ t ≤ b, both ends included. PN boutons are interpolated onto the KC-claw frame times.
      • This page is for looking. Fits here use fly-mean RDMs, so noise in RDM 1 flattens the slope; the paper corrects the bouton → claw slope for trial reliability, tests what claws beyond E[claws | boutons] tracks with a split-fly instrumental-variable fit (claws ~ boutons(IV) + reference), and takes p-values from odor-label permutations.
      • The labmate's APL matrices cover 12 of the 17 odors (missing odors in grey) and use cosine similarity. Any comparison with them uses the 66 shared pairs, and z-scores are taken over those pairs.
