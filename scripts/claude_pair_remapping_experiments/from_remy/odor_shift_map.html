Which odor groups shift, where, when — and what it means

Figure 7 analysis, updated 2026-10-22 with the labmate’s APL-silencing matrices. One row per shift in odor similarity between stages (ORN → PN boutons → KC claws → KC somata) or over time within a stage: the odor groups it moves, when it is present, the candidate mechanism, the functional reading, and what it adds to prior work. Terms: claws beyond E[claws | boutons] (claw similarity minus a fit from bouton similarity) splits into an odor part (one value per odor) and a pair part (the rest); the odor-part drift is the odor part’s change over 1–3 s; somata beyond E[somata | claws] is built the same way; the claws→somata change is the plain difference z(somata) − z(claws) per pair.
